# Notices

mise-en-scene is a Svelte 5 port of Stageshow by Benji Taylor (https://benji.org/stageshow,
https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills), which is MIT licensed. Both copyrights are in `LICENSE`.

What comes from Stageshow:

- `skill/references/craft.md`, `framing.md`, `motion.md` and `bento.md`: Stageshow's rules,
  unchanged apart from a header that maps its React kit names to this kit.
- The method in `skill/SKILL.md`: the one idea, the five failures and the workflow.
- The cursor glyph in `src/lib/Cursor.svelte`, taken from Stageshow's kit.

The Svelte components, the `Act` runner, the film script, the examples and the Facile rules are
new. Product names in the examples are illustrative.
